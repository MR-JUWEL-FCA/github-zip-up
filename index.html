What is this?

github-zip-up is a simple web tool to upload ZIP files to GitHub and manage your repositories from your phone or browser. No git commands, no installs.

  • 📦 Upload a ZIP and it unpacks straight into your repo
  • ⚡ Fast uploads that resume by themselves if your internet drops
  • 🖼️ Edit, rename, delete files, upload images and whole folders
  • 🔐 Your token never leaves your browser
